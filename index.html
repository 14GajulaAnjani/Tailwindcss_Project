Access

No barriers to knowledge.

More than a building full of books — a home for curious minds.

Greenwood Library opened its doors in 1985 with a small room and a few hundred donated books. Today it holds over twelve thousand titles across six sections.

Everyone is welcome, regardless of age, ability or background. Our staff help with research, homework, job applications and finding your next favourite novel.

We host author talks, story hours and weekend workshops, so the library is as much a meeting place as a place to borrow books.

With our digital collection, your library card works from anywhere.
